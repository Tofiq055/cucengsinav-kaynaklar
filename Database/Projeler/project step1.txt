Project – Step1

For this step, first of all you have to determine your project topic and project group members. Each group can have up to 4 students. You can choose one of the below topics for your project work.

Topics:

· Supermarket On-line Sales System

· Airline Reservation System

· An e-commerce system (i.e., electronics device sales system, bookstore, etc.)

· Bus reservation system

· For hospitals, appointment and patient registration system

· Hotel reservation system

· Student registration system

· Hotel reception and management system

· Doctor office management system

· Pharmacy management system

· Sport center management system

· Health center management system

You can use any programming language and any database management system provided that your system is Web-based.

In this step of your project work, you have to give a detailed description of your project topic including:

- Users of your system: Which kinds of users will use your system? (i.e., admin, customers, patients, receptionists, etc.)

- Describe what each user group will do by using your system.

- What is the purpose of your system?

- Entities of your system: List and describe all the entities in your system. Give attributes of the entities.

- Relationships in your system: List and describe all kind of relationships between the entities. Give attributes of the entities.

Write the name of the project topic, names and student ids of the group member, and give detailed description for the above items into a word document. Upload this document until March 13th 2015 up to 23.
